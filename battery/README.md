# Image battery

Images for judging any change to unpixel's output by eye. Compare before and after on all of them, together with `examples/axolotl/input.png`, before a change that alters pixels is published.

| Image | What it tests |
|---|---|
| `diver.png` | Strong highlights on saturated color, and the soft glow of a lantern |
| `leaves.png` | Close shades in hundreds of small leaves, and a white sticker border |
| `chef.png` | Soft outlines, close to flat art with no lines |
| `ghost.png` | Pale colors on a white background |
| `watchmaker.png` | Thin lines and very small details |
| `float.png` | Holes that must become transparent, and a white sticker border |
| `sunset.png` | Airbrushed gradients with a full-bleed background |
| `principito.jpeg` | Soft shadows on the ground, lit and shaded areas of the planet, skin tone, and two bands of sky |

The axolotl images were generated with Gemini. `sunset.png`, and faintly `leaves.png`, carry Gemini's sparkle in a corner, which is not part of the artwork.
